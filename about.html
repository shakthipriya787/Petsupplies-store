Our story

One Stall, One Rescue Dog, One Promise

In the winter of 2014, our founder Hannah Okoye took in a rescue greyhound named Biscuit who had been fed nothing but the cheapest kibble her whole life. Within a month her coat changed, her energy returned, and her vet asked what we were feeding her. That question became a business.

Hannah started selling a short, obsessive list of products — food she could trace to a named source, toys that survived Biscuit, bedding she could wash a hundred times — from a market stall at the Saturday fair. People came back because we said “I don’t know” when we didn’t know, and we called the local vet before recommending anything.

Today our studio covers 14,000 square feet, but the rule hasn’t moved. Every product is trialled by our own animals and reviewed by a veterinarian before it reaches a shelf. If we can’t stand behind it, we don’t stock it — full stop.

  • Every product trialled by our own pets — for a minimum of eight weeks at home.
  • 1% of revenue funds rescue partnerships with three local shelters.
  • Zero sponsored reviews. Paid partnerships are labelled clearly.
What drives us

Mission, Vision & the Rules We Keep

Three short statements that decide every argument we have about a product, a price or a policy.

Our Mission

To make excellent pet care affordable, understandable and joyful — for every animal and every household that opens their door to one.

Our Vision

A world where nobody has to guess what is in the bowl, the crate or the cupboard — and where adopting is always the first option people consider.

Our Promise

Honest labels, honest pricing and honest advice. If a cheaper option genuinely suits your pet, we will tell you — and you’ll still get our service.

Safety First

Choking hazards, recalled batches and toxic ingredients are tracked in real time.

No Greenwashing

We publish the full ingredient and sourcing data behind every claim.

Planet Kind

Recyclable packaging, carbon-neutral shipping and refill programmes.

Rescue Always

Adoption fairs every quarter, plus free starter kits for new adopters.

Our journey

Twelve Years, One Direction

From a Saturday market stall to a national store — with a few excellent mistakes along the way.

2014

The first Saturday stall

Forty products, one rescue greyhound, and a notebook of customer questions we promised to answer by Monday.

2016

Our first shop & vet partnership

We opened a 900 sq ft store on Willow Lane and signed a formal advisory agreement with two local veterinary practices.

2018

Online store launches

Same-day delivery within 15 miles. Our packing team hand-writes a care note in every first order.

2021

The adoption fund

1% of every order funds our shelter partners. In three years that paid for 640 rescue medical treatments.

2023

Grooming studio & refill programme

A calm, appointment-only grooming studio opened alongside a bulk refill station to cut plastic waste by 38%.

2026

40,000 pet parents

Still independently owned, still answering the phone ourselves, still saying “I don’t know” when it’s true.
