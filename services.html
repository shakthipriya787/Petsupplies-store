Service spotlight

The Calm Grooming Studio

Most dogs hate the groomer. Not because of us — because of the noise, the hurry and the strangers. So we rebuilt our studio around one idea: less is more.

Two appointment slots run at a time instead of six, each room is treated with a pheromone diffuser, and the dryer runs at a frequency most dogs stop reacting to within a session. Nervous dogs are given a fifteen-minute settling period with no handling at all, and we will happily split a full groom across two visits rather than rush your pet.

  • Breakable appointments. Pay for the time your pet actually needs, not a fixed slot.
  • Fear-free certified. Every groomer is trained in low-stress handling.
  • Photo record. Timestamped before-and-after images, kept in your pet’s file.
